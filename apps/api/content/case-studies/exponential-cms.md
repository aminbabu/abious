## Project Overview

Exponential CMS is a powerful enterprise content management system developed and maintained by **7x**. Built as a fork of eZ Publish, the platform provides organizations with a flexible and scalable solution for managing complex digital content, websites, and business workflows.

Over the past **2.5+ years**, I have been actively involved in the continuous development and improvement of the platform. Unlike a fixed-scope project, Exponential CMS has been an evolving product requiring ongoing feature development, UI improvements, client-specific customizations, maintenance, and technical enhancements.

## Goals & Objectives

The primary objective was to continuously modernize and improve the CMS while maintaining the stability and flexibility expected from an enterprise-grade platform.

Key objectives included:

- Designing intuitive and modern interfaces for both administrators and end users.
- Building responsive, accessible, and maintainable frontend components.
- Improving the overall user experience across various CMS modules.
- Supporting backend development when required for feature implementation and maintenance.
- Collaborating with multiple teams to deliver new features, client requirements, and product enhancements.

## Role & Responsibilities

My primary responsibility throughout the project has been **frontend design and implementation**. I worked extensively on transforming design concepts into responsive and maintainable interfaces while ensuring consistency across the entire CMS.

My responsibilities included:

- Designing and developing frontend interfaces for various CMS modules.
- Building reusable UI components and layouts.
- Improving usability and accessibility throughout the platform.
- Implementing responsive designs for different screen sizes.
- Integrating frontend components with backend APIs and CMS functionality.
- Assisting with backend development whenever additional support was needed.
- Fixing bugs, optimizing existing features, and participating in ongoing maintenance.

Working on a long-term product also meant regularly reviewing existing implementations and continuously refining them as the platform evolved.

## Process

Unlike many projects that have a clear beginning and end, Exponential CMS has been an ongoing journey of continuous development.

Throughout more than two and a half years, the platform has received countless updates, feature additions, client-specific enhancements, and UI improvements. Every release introduced new challenges, from redesigning legacy interfaces to integrating newly developed backend features.

One of the most interesting aspects of working on the CMS was balancing modern frontend development practices with an established enterprise codebase. Since the platform originated as a fork of eZ Publish, many existing modules required thoughtful refactoring rather than complete rewrites. This required understanding legacy architecture while introducing cleaner, more maintainable solutions.

There were also occasions where backend support became necessary. Whenever features required changes beyond the frontend, I collaborated with backend developers and contributed to server-side implementation, helping bridge the gap between design and functionality.

Because enterprise CMS platforms serve a wide variety of users and clients, every change had to be carefully tested to ensure compatibility, consistency, and reliability across the system.

## Outcome & Results

Working on Exponential CMS has been one of the longest and most impactful experiences of my career.

Over the years, I contributed to numerous interface improvements, feature implementations, and product enhancements that helped modernize the platform while preserving its enterprise capabilities.

The CMS continues to evolve, supporting various business needs through ongoing development and maintenance. My contributions helped improve usability, streamline administrative workflows, and provide a more polished experience for both content editors and administrators.

Beyond the technical achievements, this project strengthened my ability to work within a large, continuously evolving codebase while maintaining code quality and design consistency.

## Tech Stack

- **PHP** – Core language powering the CMS and backend functionality.
- **eZ Publish** – The original CMS foundation that Exponential CMS extends and customizes.
- **Symfony Components** – Used throughout the platform for routing, services, dependency injection, and application architecture.
- **Twig** – Template engine used for rendering frontend views efficiently.
- **JavaScript** – Added interactivity and dynamic functionality across the CMS.
- **SCSS** – Used for organizing and maintaining scalable styling across the application.
- **Tailwind CSS** – Utilized in newer modules to accelerate UI development and maintain design consistency.
- **REST APIs** – Enabled communication between frontend components and backend services.

## Key Learnings

Working on Exponential CMS over an extended period provided valuable professional and technical growth.

Some of the biggest lessons include:

- **Long-Term Product Development**: Learned how enterprise software evolves through continuous improvement rather than one-time delivery.
- **Legacy System Maintenance**: Gained experience working with and modernizing an established codebase without disrupting existing functionality.
- **Frontend Architecture**: Improved my ability to build scalable, reusable, and maintainable frontend components.
- **Cross-Functional Development**: Strengthened my understanding of backend systems by contributing whenever needed.
- **Collaboration**: Worked closely with designers, backend engineers, QA teams, and project managers to deliver consistent improvements.
